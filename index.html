Breaker — Campus Social App

A real-time campus social app concept for KFUPM students that turns free time between classes into spontaneous hangouts. Breaker automatically detects break times from a student's class schedule, shows friends' live availability and location on a campus map, and lets students create instant meetups or discover "Happening Now" campus events. Designed end-to-end from use case analysis and activity/sequence diagrams through to a medium-fidelity interactive Figma prototype, with a focus on usability (Nielsen's heuristics) and a clean, consistent visual language.

Darsi — On-Demand Tutor Marketplace

A mobile marketplace concept that makes finding and booking a vetted tutor as fast as ordering a ride — instead of relying on WhatsApp groups and word of mouth. Students filter tutors by subject, level, budget, and session type, compare verified profiles and reviews, then book and pay in-app with transparent, flat-fee pricing. Backed by real market research and a full business case — competitive analysis, value proposition canvas, business model canvas, 3-year financial projections, and a funding ask.
